# Selection Criteria for Master of Financial Engineering

- The programme must be a dedicated Master of Financial Engineering, Financial Engineering, or an equivalent quantitative finance degree with a clear financial-engineering focus.
- The curriculum must include core quantitative and analytical subjects such as stochastic processes, probability, optimisation, numerical methods, financial modelling, and derivative pricing.
- The degree must be recognised as a rigorous academic programme and not simply a general finance or business administration qualification.
- The programme must require a relevant prior degree, ideally in engineering, mathematics, statistics, economics, computer science, or a closely related quantitative field.
- The institution must have a credible academic reputation and strong accreditation or standing in the relevant field.
- The programme must be offered by a South African university or be easily accessible internationally.
- Exceptions may be considered in rare cases where the programme demonstrates exceptional quality, industry relevance, or international recognition.
