# Selection Criteria for Master of Computer Science

- The degree title must be explicitly Computer Science; IT-related or non-matching titles will not be accepted.
- The programme must not require Physical Science on the NSC as a mandatory admission requirement.
- Exceptions may be considered in rare cases where the university offers exceptional academic quality or strong international standing.
- The programme must require a relevant prior degree.
- The programme must be offered by a South African university or be easily accessible internationally.
